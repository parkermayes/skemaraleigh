Questions people actually ask

Do I need an idea to apply?
For the accelerator, yes: something you have already started poking at. If you are still looking for the idea, join the club first; that is exactly what it is for, and the next cohort will pull from it.
Do I need a team or a registered company?
No. Solo founders are welcome and nothing needs to be incorporated. You need a problem you care about and the willingness to talk to strangers about it.
How much time is this?
Fifty minutes a week in the room, Tuesdays 11:30–12:20. The work between sessions is whatever your venture needs. The founders who get the most out of it treat it like a class they actually want to pass.
What happens at Demo Day?
November 17. Every venture in the cohort presents real traction to students, faculty, mentors and the local Raleigh ecosystem, streamed live to Lille, Paris, Sophia, Suzhou and Belo Horizonte.
What if I still have questions?
Come to the Accelerator Interest Session on Tuesday, September 1, 11:30–12:30 in the Venture II AI Lab. It exists for exactly this. Or join the mailing list below and reply to anything that lands.
